privacy policy

last updated: 29 September 2026

Alegienne ("we", "us", "our") is a small, hand-made apothecary and floral studio based in Australia. this policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your information across alegienne.com.au, including florah, plume and our email list.

information we collect

when you order, subscribe, or write to us — by email, through this site, or via a checkout link — we collect what you give us: your name, email address, delivery details, and anything you tell us about your order.

if you purchase through a third-party checkout (currently Square), that provider processes your payment directly. we do not collect or store your card details ourselves.

if you subscribe to our email list, we collect your email address and your topic preferences (events & updates, and/or the lunar love note), stored with our email provider, Resend.

our website host may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and performance — this is not used to identify you personally.

how we use it

we use your information to prepare and deliver your order, reply to your enquiry, send the emails you've opted into, and meet our legal and accounting obligations. we do not sell, rent or trade your information to anyone.

third parties

your information may pass through our email provider (Resend) and our payment/checkout provider (Square), each handling your data under their own privacy practices.

data retention & security

we keep order, enquiry and subscriber details only as long as needed to fulfil your order, meet our record-keeping obligations, or until you unsubscribe. we take reasonable care to protect your information, though no method of transmission over the internet is ever completely secure.

your rights

you can unsubscribe from any of our emails at any time via the link in the email, or by writing to us. you may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time, by contacting us below. this policy is informed by the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and, where relevant, comparable laws such as the GDPR.

changes

we may update this policy from time to time. the current version always lives on this page.

contact

questions about your information: orders@alegienne.com.au
